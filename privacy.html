Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Google OAuth app described on the home page. The app is used only by its owner, to reach the owner's own Google account.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app may access the owner's Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Tasks. It uses this access only to carry out actions the owner starts, or schedules on the owner's own machines. For example, it lists or files the owner's mail and writes encrypted backup files to the owner's Drive.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed only on computers the owner controls, and stored only there and in the owner's own Google account.
  • Backups are encrypted with rclone crypt before they leave the owner's server. Google stores only encrypted contents and encrypted file names.
  • OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's machines and are never shared.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party. It is not used for advertising, analytics or training machine-learning models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other users

The app has no users besides its owner. It does not collect data about anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings.

Contact

Questions can be raised through github.com/ixtli.
